# Drama Studio Companion privacy policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026. Applies to Drama Studio Companion 0.4 and
later for Chrome and Edge.

Drama Studio Companion is a browser extension that works with the Drama Studio
desktop app on your own computer. It has no server and no account, and the
publisher collects nothing.

## What the Companion handles, and why

| Data                                                                                 | Why                                          | Where it goes                                                  |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Prompts, song lyrics and titles you send from Drama Studio or type in the side panel | To type them into the AI site you chose      | Only into that site's page, in your own browser tab            |
| Reference pictures and clips you pick in Drama Studio                                | To add them to that site's own upload button | Only to that site, the same way as if you picked them yourself |
| Pictures, videos and songs you choose with **Send to Drama Studio**                  | To put them in your Drama Studio project     | Only to the Drama Studio app on this computer                  |
| Grok Imagine results in a batch you start                                            | To save them to your Downloads folder        | Your Downloads folder                                          |
| Settings (modes, delays, download folder and naming)                                 | To remember your choices                     | The extension's storage in your browser                        |
| An activity log                                                                      | So you can copy it when you report a problem | Kept in the side panel only; it holds no prompts or files      |

## What the Companion never does

- It never sends anything to the publisher or any other server. It has no
  analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking.
- It never sells or shares data, and never uses it for anything other than
  the feature you used.
- It never presses Send, Generate or Create for you on an AI site, except in
  a Grok Imagine batch that you start yourself, and it stops at once at any
  sign-in page, usage limit, moderation message or CAPTCHA. It never solves a
  CAPTCHA or works around a limit.
- It never reads your passwords, payment details or other sites' pages.

## Connections

- **The AI sites you use** (Google Flow, Grok Imagine, Kling AI, Runway,
  Hailuo AI, Luma Dream Machine, Pika, ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity,
  Manus, Midjourney, Ideogram, Leonardo and Suno), only on pages you open.
  What each site does with your prompts and files is covered by that site's
  own terms and privacy policy.
- **The Drama Studio app on this computer**, through Chrome's native
  messaging. This is optional: Chrome asks you the first time you connect,
  and Drama Studio asks you to allow the browser. You can remove it in
  Drama Studio › Settings › Browser at any time.

## Permissions

- `storage`: your settings.
- `tabs`: to find and focus the AI site tab a job or batch belongs to.
- `downloads`: to save batch results to your Downloads folder.
- `contextMenus`: the **Send to Drama Studio** item on pictures, videos and
  songs from the supported sites.
- `sidePanel`: the Companion's panel.
- Host access to `grok.com`, `imgen.x.ai` and `vidgen.x.ai`: Grok Imagine
  batches and downloads.
- Optional host access to each other site's media hosts: asked one site at a
  time, the first time you send a result from that site to Drama Studio.
- Optional `nativeMessaging`: the link to the Drama Studio app, asked when you
  connect.

## Removing data

Remove the extension from `chrome://extensions` to delete its settings.
Downloaded files stay in your Downloads folder, and files you sent stay in
your Drama Studio project.

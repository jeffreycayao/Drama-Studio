Top findings

What to fix first, in the order I would do it.

Broken or misleading

  1. Send Feedback goes nowhere. The form looks finished, but nothing is sent. Hide it, or point it at the support email, until the feedback service exists.
  2. The Assistant shows raw tool output. "TOOL RESULT: { … }" blocks fill the chat. Collapse them behind a "details" toggle by default.

Hidden features

  1. The strongest AI tools are right-click only: Continue from last frame, Lip-sync, Smart Replace, Translate captions, Detect beats. Put them in the Inspector quick actions and a visible "AI" menu on the clip.
  2. Pro editing tools are right-click only: masks, compound clips, paste attributes, match frame, move by offset, label colours.
  3. The prompt library sits in the bottom-right menu and in small "Library" buttons. Give it a top-level place, next to Cast.
  4. Setup Doctor and the guided tour sit two menus deep. Offer them on the welcome screen and in Help.
  5. Import pack only shows in Generate › My Workflows. Add it to the Assets panel import menu.

Controls that need a redesign

  1. Timeline toolbar: about 30 tiny buttons in one row, with tools (Move, Trim, Razor, Slip, Slide) mixed with actions (Split, Copy, Delete). Split it into a tool group, an add group (Text, Shape, Captions, Music) and an overflow menu, and add labels on wide screens.
  2. Top bar: 12 items compete. The gold Learn AI button is louder than Save or Export. Group Cast, Credits and ComfyUI into one status area, and keep one primary action.
  3. "Delete: Delete Disk / Project Only" sits permanently at the top of Assets. It's a risky choice shown all the time. Move it into the delete confirmation instead.
  4. Track headers: unlabeled S, M, P and lock buttons, and track names cut off ("C…", "Vi…"). Use clearer icons with tooltips and a wider header.
  5. Idea Pad opens on an empty canvas. Offer starter templates: "Three-act short", "Micro-drama episode".

Under-used or not finished

  1. Discover tab: tutorials only, separate from Help. Merge it into the Help Center as "Watch".
  2. ComfyUI tab: a full node editor that most creators shouldn't need. Label it "Advanced" and keep it out of the beginner flow.
  3. Companion extension and browser link: Generate › AI Sites now sends prompts and references to 12 sites, and "Send to Drama Studio" brings results back. Grok Imagine now has a Send to Drama Studio button on each result and sets the shape itself; the other 11 sites are still right-click only and shape by hand. Each site needs a live check, since site pages change.
  4. Motion Studio: built. One guided "AI does the edit" flow on its own top tab. Next: a storyboard view and checks that look at the rendered cut.
  5. Hidden workspaces: the Agent, Flow AI and MoGraph tabs exist in the app but are switched off in the top bar. MoGraph (animated titles) could feed Motion Studio's title cards.
  6. Help Center in Japanese: the UI is translated, but the guides are not.

Top findings

What to fix first, in the order I would do it.

Fixed in the UX review pass (Unreleased)

  1. Send Feedback no longer pretends to send: until the feedback service exists it points to Discord and the website, with the version to include.
  2. The Assistant folds tool steps into one quiet row and hides any raw tool output behind a Details toggle.
  3. AI and pro tools are visible: an AI button on the selected clip and grouped Inspector quick actions (AI, Captions, Audio, Edit tools, label colours).
  4. Prompts sit next to Cast in the top bar. Setup and Take the tour are on the welcome screen and the Help home. Import › Workflow pack is in the Assets panel.
  5. Timeline toolbar regrouped: tools, Add (+ menu), Split and Delete, Snap and Ripple, More. Labels when there is room.
  6. Top bar: one status box (Cast, Prompts, Credits, jobs, ComfyUI), a quiet Learn AI link, and Export as the one lime button.
  7. Delete choice moved into the delete confirmation (project only by default).
  8. Track headers: icons with tooltips for show, solo, mute, lock and target track; wider headers.
  9. Idea Pad offers Three-act short and Micro-drama episode templates.
  10. Discover is now Help › Watch. ComfyUI is labelled Advanced. Help guides are in Japanese.

Still open

  1. Companion per-site checks: AI Sites sends prompts and references to 12 sites; only Grok Imagine has the on-page Send button and sets the shape itself. The other 11 need a live check each, since site pages change.
  2. Motion Studio: next is a storyboard view and checks that look at the rendered cut.
  3. Hidden workspaces: Agent stays off (the Assistant panel replaces it). Flow AI and MoGraph stay off until finished; MoGraph could later feed Motion Studio's title cards.
  4. Feedback service: build and deploy it, then the in-app form comes back automatically.
  5. Watch has no videos yet: the tutorial catalog is empty.
  6. New labels (clip AI menu, toolbar groups, Idea Pad templates, Assets import menu) are English in the Japanese UI, like the surrounding panels.
